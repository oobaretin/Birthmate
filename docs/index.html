Birthmate Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026
App: Birthmate (com.birthmate.app)

Overview

Birthmate helps you explore famous people and historical events associated with your birth month and day. This policy describes what data the app uses and how it is handled.

Data we collect

On your device (local)

  • Birth month and day — stored in app preferences on your device to personalize content.
  • Favorites — birthmates you save are stored locally.
  • Notification preference — whether daily reminders are enabled and your chosen reminder time.
  • Welcome tour seen — a flag so the intro sheet is not shown every launch.

Optional community features (when enabled)

If you sign in with Apple and use Birthday Circle (requires a future app update with live sign-in):

  • Apple user identifier — used to authenticate you with our backend (Supabase).
  • Display name — shown to other Circle members if you opt in.
  • Birth month and day — shared with Circle; birth year is never uploaded.
  • Optional famous twin name — if you choose to set one on your profile.

Data we do not collect

  • Precise location
  • Contacts or photos (unless you use system share sheets yourself)
  • Birth year (never uploaded to Circle)

Third-party services

  • Wikipedia / Wikidata — the app fetches public “on this day” and birth information. Requests go to Wikimedia APIs; their policies apply.
  • Supabase (optional) — hosts Birthday Circle account and profile data when community sign-in is enabled.

How we use data

  • Personalize Today, Birthmates, and History for your date
  • Save your favorites and settings on device
  • Enable optional social features when you sign in
  • Send local daily reminders if you enable notifications

Data retention & deletion

  • Local data: delete the app from your device to remove on-device preferences and favorites.
  • Change birthday: use Settings → Change Birthday to reset your date and return to onboarding.
  • Circle account: when live sign-in is available, contact the developer to request account deletion, or use in-app account removal when offered.

Children

Birthmate is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Community data is transmitted over HTTPS. Sign in with Apple is used when live community features are enabled.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
